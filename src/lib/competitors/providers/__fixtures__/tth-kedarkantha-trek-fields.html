How to reach —Kedarkantha Trek Maps

How to Reach Dehradun
Dehradun to Sankari

You’ll have to arrive in Dehradun, the capital of Uttarakhand. This is the main pickup point for this trek by TTH, from where the trek journey begins.

Options to Reach Dehradun

There are many ways to reach Dehradun, but the most convenient option is to arrive directly at the Dehradun bus station, airport, or railway station, depending on your preference.

  • Arrival by Bus

    • If you are taking a bus, it is highly convenient to catch a direct overnight bus from Delhi.

    • From Delhi: Reach the ISBT Kashmiri Gate in Delhi and take a direct Volvo or sleeper bus to ISBT Dehradun, which takes around 5 to 6 hours to reach via the Delhi-Dehradun Expressway.

    (We highly recommend choosing government Uttarakhand Transport Corporation (UTC) buses over private ones, as they are generally more reliable. Ensure you reach Dehradun by 6:00 AM on the pickup day).

  • Arrival by Air

    • For international and domestic trekkers opting for a flight, the most preferred option is to fly into Jolly Grant Airport (Dehradun Airport).

    Location Travelling Guide / Travel Instruction
    DELHI The Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi, offers multiple daily direct flights to Jolly Grant Airport (Dehradun). Major airlines include IndiGo, Alliance Air, and Air India.
    OUTSTATION
    (Mumbai / Blr / Hyd / Pune)     		You can book a direct or connecting flight to Dehradun Airport. If direct flights are unavailable, flying via Delhi is the best route.

    From Dehradun Airport to the Pickup Point: The distance between Jolly Grant Airport and Dehradun city center is around 25-30 km. After arriving, you can easily take a public bus or book a private cab to reach the TTH pickup point.

    Note: If you are arriving by air, it is highly recommended to reach Dehradun one day in advance.

  • Arrival by Train

    • Dehradun is well-connected by rail via the Dehradun Railway Station (DDN).

    • Direct Trains: High-speed trains like the Dehradun Shatabdi Express, Vande Bharat Express, and Mussoorie Express run daily from New Delhi. Several other cities, including Kolkata (Howrah), Varanasi, Mumbai, and others, have direct train services to Dehradun. If you’re travelling from any of these routes, you can take a direct train to Dehradun.
    • To the pickup point: If your train terminates here, you can take a short auto-rickshaw or local cab ride directly to the pickup point. To know more about the train schedules, you can visit the IRCTC website.

Pick Up Information

  • Trekkers who wish to opt for the pickup and drop-off service can select this option during the booking process under the "View Add-ons" section.
  • Trekkers need to assemble at Prince Chowk, Dehradun, at 6:00 AM for the Trek.
  • If you have opted for TTH’s pickup and drop-off service, your travel from the Dehradun assembly point to the mountain base camp will be completely managed by TTH.

Drop-Off Information

  • The designated drop-off point is Prince Chowk, Dehradun.
  • The drop-off is scheduled on the final day between 6:30 PM to 7:30 PM. We firmly advise all trekkers to plan their onward travel arrangements (trains or flights) after 8:00 PM.
  • We strongly recommend keeping a buffer day in your travel plan. Mountain roads in the Uttarkashi region are prone to sudden landslides and delays. If everything goes as planned, you can use that buffer day to explore Dehradun or nearby Mussoorie.
  • As part of our transport service, TTH offers comfortable transportation modes such as Tempo Travelers, Boleros, or equivalent vehicles.
  • If you wish to upgrade your private mode of transportation, you can reach out to your designated trek coordinator for further assistance.
  • It's highly advisable to keep a buffer day in your travel plan. If the buffer day is not needed, it can be used to explore Dehradun/Mussoorie/Rishikesh.

( If you prefer to travel independently to Base camp and don't want to take TTH's pick-up service, you can either take a government bus or book a private cab from Dehradun. Your trek coordinator will provide guidance on how to arrange for the bus or cab booking. )
