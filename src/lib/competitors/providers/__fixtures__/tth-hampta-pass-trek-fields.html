Hampta Pass Trek Itinerary

Day 1

Drive from Manali to Jobra and Trek from Jobra to Chhika

  • Altitude:
    Manali - 2,050 m / 6,730 ft
    Jobra - 2,800 m / 9,300 ft
    Chhika- 3,100 m / 10,100 ft.
  • Drive Distance (Manli to Jobra): 16km | Duration: 1hr.
  • Trek Distance (Jobra to Chhika): 3km | Duration: 2hrs.
  • Altitude Gain: 3,370 ft.
  • Cloakroom facilities is available at Basecamp Manali

    • Note: Reporting Time: 12:00 PM, Place: The Woodbine, Naggar Rd, Manali, Drop Time: 6:30 pm to 7:30 pm (Timings are subject to change based on weather and road conditions). It’s advisable to reach Manali a day before to avoid any kind of delays.

Day 2

Trek from Chhika to Balu Ka Ghera

  • Altitude (Balu Ka Ghera): 3,600 m / 12,000 ft.
  • Trek Distance: 7km | Duration: 5-6 hrs.
  • Altitude Gain: 1,900 ft
Day 3

Trek from Balu Ka Ghera to Shea Goru via Hampta Pass

  • Altitude:
    • Hampta Pass - 4,300 m / 14,100 ft
    • Shea Goru- 3,900 m/ 12,900 ft
  • Trek Distance: 7-8 km | Duration: 9 - 10 hrs
  • Altitude Gain: 2,100 ft; Then loss of 1,200 ft
Day 4

Trek from Shea Goru to Chatru and Drive to Chandratal and back to Chatru

  • Altitude:
    • Chatru- 3,350 m / 11,000 ft.
    • Chandratal - 4,300 m / 14,100 ft
  • Trek Distance: 7 km | Duration: 4-5 hr.
  • Drive Distance: 70 km | Duration: 4-5 hrs to
  • Altitude gain of 1,200 ft and then loss of 3,100 ft.

Note: The drive to Chandratal and back to the campsite will be done either on day 4 or day 5, depending on road and weather conditions.

Day 5

Drive from Chatru to Manali

  • Altitude (Manali): 2,050 m / 6,730 ft
  • Drive Distance: 65 km | Duration: 4-5 hr.
  • Altitude Loss: 4,270 ft.

How to Reach Manali

Pickup Information

If you choose TTH’s pickup service, TTH will manage the rest of your travel arrangements once you have reached the assembly point of the trek in Manali. You can select this option during the booking process by adding it as an add-on.

To reach Hampta Pass, you have to first travel to Manali, which is the main base camp. Before that, you can arrive at Chandigarh, Delhi, Pathankot, or Bhuntar airport.

Please Note: The most ideal place to reach Manali is from Chandigarh.

  • Arrival by Bus:

    • If you are taking a bus for Manali, then try to take the bus from Chandigarh, as it’s more convenient.

    • To take the bus from Chandigarh, first reach the Inter-State Bus Terminus (ISBT) in Sector 43. From there you can take a direct overnight bus to Manali, which will take approx. 6 - 10 hours to reach.
    • According to the HRTC schedule, the first bus for Manali departs at 3:40 AM and the last one runs until 9:00 PM. Although, we highly recommend that you visit the HRTC website to check the bus schedule according to your travel plan.
    • From Delhi, reach the ISBT Kashmiri Gate and take a direct bus to Manali, which takes around 14 hours to reach. A pro tip: numerous buses leave for Manali throughout the day, but departures peak between 6:00 PM and 11:55 PM.

    (We highly recommend choosing government buses over private ones; based on our experience, private buses are more prone to delays.) Government buses are generally more reliable. Whichever option you choose, ensure you reach Manali by 10:00 AM on the pickup day.

  • Arrival by Air:

    • For all the international trekkers opting for flight, it is the most preferred option to reach Bhuntar Airport (Kullu-Manali Airport).

    Location Travelling Guide / Travel Instruction
    DELHI
    • The Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi, is the prime airport that offers a direct flight to Bhuntar Airport.
    • As for the airlines, the major airline that offers a direct flight to Manali is the “Alliance Air”. Other than that Air India, IndiGo, and others also operate on this route.
    • From Delhi Airport, you can also opt to travel by bus as mentioned above.
    CHANDIGARH
    • Since Chandigarh is a better option and it takes less time from here then you can book a connecting flight to Chandigarh via Delhi/Hyd/Pune/Mumbai and then book a cab or take a bus to reach Manali.
    • The distance between the Chandigarh Airport and Chandigarh Bus Stand is 14 km.

    After arriving at the Bhuntar Airport (Kullu-Manali Airport), you can either take a bus or book a cab to reach the pickup point (Woodbine Hotel).

    Note— The flights to Bhuntar are highly dependent on the weather conditions and seasonality. So, we firmly advise you to arrive a day or two before your trek.

  • Arrival By Train:

    • There is no railway station in Manali, so there’s no direct train that goes there. The nearest major railway stations are: Joginder Nagar (145 – 160 km), Chandigarh (260 - 270 km), Ambala Cantt (320 – 330 km), and Pathankot (390 – 400 km).

    From a connectivity point of view, Chandigarh railway station is ideal for reaching Manali. To know more about the train schedule, you can visit the IRCTC website. From Chandigarh Railway Station you need to travel to ISBT Sector 43 (approximately 10–11 km), and then you can take an overnight bus to Manali.

Note: If you prefer to travel on your own, you can choose between a government bus or a private cab. Your trek coordinator will assist you with all the necessary details and guidance for arranging the bus or cab booking.

Drop-Off Information

  • The designated drop-off point is the Woodbine Hotel in Manali. around 6:30 pm to 7:30 pm.
  • Please consider planning your subsequent travel arrangements after 8:00 pm.
  • It's highly advisable to keep a buffer day in your travel plan. If the buffer day is not needed, it can be used to explore Manali.
  • TTH offers comfortable transportation through Tempo Traveler, Bolero, or equivalent vehicles. If you wish to upgrade your mode of transportation, please contact your trek coordinator for further assistance.
